Owner & Founder: Tran Dinh Tuan

HaKai Art Agency was founded by Tran Dinh Tuan with a vision to honor aesthetics and limitless creative power. We take pride in crafting stunning visual experiences across Digital Drawing, 3D Rendering, and high-end Motion Graphics.
